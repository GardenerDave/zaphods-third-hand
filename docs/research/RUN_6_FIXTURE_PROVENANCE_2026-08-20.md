# Run 6 fixture provenance

These fixtures are independently authored for the validation-gated conditional
economic-escalation experiment. They cover fresh triage-routing and
scope-authority-boundary scenarios with bounded review-only authority.

The scenarios vary evidence routing, ownership, approval, target scope,
staleness, and cross-artifact authority conflicts. They were authored without
consulting model outputs and are not replacements or paraphrases of prior
Run 1–5 prompts.
