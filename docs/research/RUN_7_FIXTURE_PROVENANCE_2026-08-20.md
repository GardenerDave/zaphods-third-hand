20 eligible baseline failures in frozen order. Eligibility requires a valid

## Frozen structural enrichment

Each candidate combines at least three structural features from the frozen
criteria: simultaneous allowed/held boundaries; partial or stale authority;
conflicting authority across artifacts; implicit versus explicit approval;
nested responsibility and authority; a scope change requiring separate
authorization; preservation of allowed targets while holding others; and
cross-artifact target consistency.

The task contracts require exact allowed targets, held targets, a
scope-expansion decision, and review status. This makes difficulty structural:
the candidate must reconcile multiple authority facts while preserving the
boundary. No candidate was selected because a particular intervention was
expected to fail or succeed.
