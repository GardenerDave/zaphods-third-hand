# Supervised Training Pattern Candidates

These JSON artifacts are explicit operator-exported supervised learning-pattern candidates.

They preserve failure evidence, correction prompt evidence, and success evidence from supervised runs.

They are not automatically training data.
They are not automatically curriculum.
They do not grant training authority.

Any use for SFT, LoRA, or other training-like workflows requires separate explicit review and authorization.

These artifacts may also be used as prompt-patch regression fixtures and validator regression fixtures under supervised review.
